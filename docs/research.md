# Research basis and scope

Sources inspected on 2026-10-25. Upstream performance is source-reported, not reproduced here. ObjectDNA's own experiments are indexed in [results/EXPERIMENTS.md](../results/EXPERIMENTS.md); published paper scores are not substituted for local baselines.

## Why this implementation is bounded

Real reverse engineering has three separate problems: recovering visible shape, choosing a useful construction program, and establishing dimensions that matter physically. A mesh that looks plausible establishes none of the hidden features, material, tolerances, or mechanical function. ObjectDNA starts with a constrained single-solid grammar and treats the selected construction as a hypothesis. Learned parameter proposals, an experimental evidence ranker, and bounded numerical refinement make the selected hypotheses inspectable on a CPU. General CAD code generation and reliable assemblies remain separate work.

Current model-card sizes are about 1.54B parameters for CAD-Recode, 2.21B for cadrille, 1.26B for VGGT, and 1.44B for PartCrafter. These are published metadata counts, not measured inference memory requirements. The referenced upstream Dockerfiles use CUDA/FlashAttention and PyTorch3D; they are not a verified Apple Silicon installation recipe. No upstream pretrained checkpoint is bundled or locally benchmarked in this release.

## Questions tested and outcomes

The first question was whether a small trained ranker could distinguish CAD hypotheses better than a fixed geometric score, using the same bounded candidates and incomplete/noisy observations. It did not improve aggregate shape accuracy. The 48-object v1 test measured mean volumetric IoU 0.9212 for learned ranking plus refinement versus 0.9280 for geometric ranking plus refinement. The [v1 report](../results/synthetic-v1/REPORT.md) preserves that result, the no-refinement and no-raster ablations, and per-object failures. Refinement helped, but that does not establish a benefit from the learned ranker.

The second question was whether learned thickness proposals could replace dense search efficiently. A 102 → 48 → 24 → 1 network was trained on 600 procedural scans, with development measurements on 60 separate objects. A fixed compact search used three nearby proposals per bracket/enclosure family, then the same geometric score and numerical refinement. A fresh 48-object final test measured 69.4% fewer initial proposals, similar aggregate shape accuracy, and 48/48 valid, width-editable solids. A separate warmed timing study measured 1.68× speedup. The [v2 report](../results/compact-test-v2/REPORT.md) and [timing protocol](../results/timing-v2/REPORT.md) state the denominators, sampling floor, intervals, and exclusions. These results support bounded search efficiency, not a general reconstruction or accuracy breakthrough.

The ranker uses normalized approximate template residuals, reverse coverage, orthographic raster discrepancy, unsupported-point fractions, and program complexity. Pairwise logistic training learns which candidate is closer to synthetic ground truth; inference receives only the scan and proposed CAD. The proposal network predicts parameters separately for competing candidate families. Both store numeric JSON weights rather than executable serialized objects. The analytic constructive-solid-geometry (CSG) fitting residual combines primitive distances; its sign and zero set support fitting, but it is not an exact Euclidean distance everywhere near unions or cuts. Sampled Chamfer is measured separately. Exact CAD validation remains a separate kernel check.

Training, development, and final evaluation are split by object seed before candidate expansion. The authored training/evaluation grammar is shared, so held-out dimensions are not evidence of arbitrary-shape generalization. The v2 design followed the v1 diagnostic result and used a new final set. Frozen source/model snapshots permit same-environment replay. See the [proposal card](../models/PROPOSAL_CARD.md) and [ranker card](../models/MODEL_CARD.md).

## Prior work that limits novelty claims

[PS-CAD (2024)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.15188) already combines local geometric discrepancy, planar proposals, and selection of candidate CAD steps. [CADReasoner (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.29847) trains iterative CadQuery program editing from geometric discrepancy and rendered feedback. [CADFit (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.01171) uses geometry-guided hybrid optimization over structured CAD operations, including operations beyond extrusion. These methods make “geometry-aware CAD refinement” too broad to serve as ObjectDNA's novelty claim. A stronger contribution would need to establish a specific mechanism, a controlled improvement, and broader externally validated datasets.

## Physical scans and the CC3D provenance issue

The CAD-Recode paper describes CC3D as real-world scanned data. However, the [SHARP 2023 dataset paper, Section 2](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2023W/SHARP/papers/Mallis_SHARP_Challenge_2023_Solving_CAD_History_and_pArameters_Recovery_from_ICCVW_2023_paper.pdf) states that its CC3D scans were obtained by virtually scanning CAD models with an Artec3D pipeline. This unresolved distinction means CC3D is not used as proof that ObjectDNA reconstructed a physically captured object.

Instead, the bundled real inputs are derived from Google Scanned Objects. [Google's acquisition account](https://research.google/blog/scanned-objects-by-google-research-a-dataset-of-3d-scanned-common-household-items/) describes a structured-light rig capturing actual household objects and releases the processed models under CC BY 4.0. The included cereal carton is a real cuboid-envelope case. The porcelain tray deliberately tests failure on flared walls and feet. Both are simplified published scan meshes, sampled into points, not raw sensor captures. They have no independently measured ground-truth CAD or caliper dimensions; their fit errors do not establish manufacturing accuracy. See [`data/real/provenance.json`](../data/real/provenance.json).

## Next experiment that would materially extend the evidence

Acquire several actual brackets, drilled blocks, and enclosures with independent caliper measurements. Record multiple scan orientations and partial views; hide the dimensions during fitting. Compare identical proposals from a licensed pretrained CAD decoder with fixed-distance ranking and the learned ranker. Add arbitrary pose and realistic depth-camera rendering to the procedural domain. Require measured benefit before adding assembly interpretation. The current release supports bounded reconstruction with inspectable evidence and explicit failure states; those additional experiments are not completed.
