## Bundled real scan derivatives

`data/real/cereal_box.xyz`, `porcelain_tray.xyz`, associated source thumbnails, `metadata.pbtxt`, and `model.config` files derive from **Google Scanned Objects, Copyright 2020 Google LLC.**, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The source items are:

- [Van's Cereal Honey Nut Crunch 11 oz box](https://app.gazebosim.org/GoogleResearch/fuel/models/Vans_Cereal_Honey_Nut_Crunch_11_oz_box), version 1.
- [Threshold rectangular porcelain tray](https://app.gazebosim.org/GoogleResearch/fuel/models/Threshold_Tray_Rectangle_Porcelain), version 1.

Changes: area-weighted surface sampling, unit conversion from SI metres to millimetres, and plain XYZ serialization. No claim of endorsement. Model names identify source objects and do not confer trademark rights. Exact archive, mesh, and derived-point hashes, source URLs, processing details, and accuracy limitations are recorded in [`data/real/provenance.json`](../data/real/provenance.json). Recreate them with `python scripts/fetch_real.py`; only these two bounded versioned downloads are fetched and their reviewed hashes must match.
